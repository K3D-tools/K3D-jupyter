===========
Support K3D
===========

K3D-jupyter is free software under the MIT licence, and it stays that way.
Nothing on this page unlocks a feature - every release contains everything.

What the options below pay for is maintainer time: keeping K3D working with new
Jupyter, browser and GPU releases, answering issues and building what comes next.
K3D is installed from PyPI more than two thousand times a day and is looked after
by a very small team.

.. |company| replace:: Artur Trzęsiok CubeProject.pl

-----------------------
Help that costs nothing
-----------------------

* **Cite K3D** when it produced a figure in your paper or report - the
  `citation metadata`_ and the DOI `10.5281/zenodo.3247652`_ always point at the
  newest release.
* **Tell us how you use it.** We see download counts, not people. One line about
  your lab, team or product - by e-mail or in an issue - shapes the roadmap and
  strengthens grant applications.
* **Report bugs** with a minimal example, and send fixes if you can.

The included time covers what usually blocks a team: getting K3D to work in your
environment (JupyterHub, HPC, air-gapped installs, CI and rendering without a
display), reviewing how you use it, and moving the fixes you depend on to the
front of the queue.

A plan is a response-time commitment, not a guaranteed fix time. Fixes made under
a plan are released to everyone.

------------------
Sponsored features
------------------

If K3D does not do something you need, you can fund it. We agree on the scope and
a fixed price before any work starts; the result is released under MIT for
everyone, and you are credited in the release notes if you want to be.

Requests so far have included rendering on servers and in CI, embedding plots in
web applications and dashboards, larger volumes and point clouds, and new export
formats.

----------------
Integration help
----------------

Hands-on help putting K3D into your product or pipeline: embedding the standalone
JavaScript build, rendering without a display, performance on your data.
Billed at €100 per hour, or as a fixed price for a defined scope.

------------------
Grant applications
------------------

Writing a proposal that depends on K3D? We can provide a letter of support, or
take part as a subcontractor for the visualisation work. K3D itself started in
the Horizon 2020 project OpenDreamKit_.

------------
How to order
------------

Write to support@k3d-jupyter.org with the name of your organisation and what you
need. Invoices are issued by |company|, registered in Poland (EU); payment is by
bank transfer and purchase orders are fine. Prices exclude VAT. Plans run for
twelve months and do not renew automatically.
