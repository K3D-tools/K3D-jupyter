k3d browser performance

Every scene in the visual suite, in every renderer mode the bundle knows. Two vsync-free probes give the cost of one copy, and from that the copies needed to make a frame cost the target - copies being scene-graph clones that share the original's geometry, so making them is free. The oldest bundle goes first and its copy count is then imposed on the rest, so one scene is always the same workload everywhere. Nothing starts until you press the button. Keep this window visible and in front - a background tab gets no animation frames and the numbers would be fiction.
